My Experiments

Personal experiments, pop-culture challenges and unusual claims tested in real life, with the process documented from baseline to result.

The idea

Do the experiment. Keep the receipts.

The point is not to manufacture dramatic before-and-after content. Each experiment starts with a question, a defined protocol and a baseline, then records what actually happened, including failure, discomfort, unexpected results and limits.

First experiment · 30 Days

The 36-eggs challenge

The first planned experiment is a 30-day challenge built around the famous old-school bodybuilding idea of consuming 36 eggs a day. TYR will document the baseline, protocol, daily experience, measurable changes and final result rather than assuming the claim is true.

Future experiments

Culture, fitness, habits and curiosity.

Future experiments can come from pop culture, old claims, modern trends, fitness challenges, routines, technology or any question interesting enough to test honestly.

Personal experiment, not general advice.

Health-related experiments document Trevor’s own experience. They are not presented as medical guidance or proof that another person should repeat the protocol.
