Privacy

Privacy

The site should collect less, explain more and never pretend a third-party service does not exist.

Current pre-launch state

This build does not operate a TYR newsletter signup, public contact form or TYR analytics script. GitHub Pages and any services used to deliver the site may process technical request information under their own policies.

Embedded services

When TYR later embeds video, newsletter or analytics services, this page will identify the material services and what they do. The site will avoid unnecessary advertising technology by default.

Contact information

No private phone number, home address or sensitive operational information is published through this site. The public TYR contact route will be activated with the launch configuration.

This page is deliberately specific to the current deployment. It will be updated when data collection changes.
