Why it comes first

The work begins with a life being lived.

Projects, experiments, trading and published reporting do not happen in a vacuum. Place, movement, family, relationships, setbacks and changing circumstances shape what Trevor notices and what becomes worth pursuing next.

Travel & movement

Go where life takes the story.

Road stories, field travel, Sierra Leone, the United States and other places that become part of the journey.

People & place

Places matter because people do.

Communities, conversations, encounters and the local details that make a place more than scenery.

Family & transitions

The context usually edited out.

Selective first-person writing about responsibility, change, setbacks, family and the choices behind the work.

Life & Places is not a diary.

A personal piece still needs a reason to exist: a revealing experience, a useful question, a meaningful place, a documented change or context that helps readers understand the larger story.
