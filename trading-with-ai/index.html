Trading with AI

Testing what artificial intelligence can and cannot contribute to real trading work, while leaving room to explore trading or AI separately when the question calls for it.

The focus

Use the tools. Test the judgment.

This pillar follows how Trevor uses AI around market research, idea generation, analysis, journaling, testing and trading discipline. The goal is not to present AI as an automatic trading system or to turn outcomes into promises.

AI

Together

Trading with AI.

The intersection: using AI inside a real trading workflow and documenting whether it improves the process rather than assuming it improves results.

Documenting a process, not offering financial advice.
