About Trevor

Trevor Young Reporting is where I document the life I’m living, the things I’m trying, the projects I’m building, and the work I decide is worth publishing.

I’ve spent years moving between Sierra Leone and the United States, business and public-interest work, technology and real-world operations. I’m interested in what happens between an idea and the result, especially the parts that are usually edited out.

Trevor Young Reporting gives those different parts of life one home without pretending they are all the same thing.

The five pillars

Life & Places comes first. Travel, people, family, transitions and place provide the context for everything else.

My Experiments is where I test personal challenges, pop-culture ideas and unusual claims in real life and document what happens.

My Projects follows the organizations, ventures and initiatives I am actively working on, currently centered largely in Sierra Leone.

Trading with AI follows my work at the intersection of trading and artificial intelligence, while leaving room for trading-only or AI-only questions when they belong.

Published Work is the editorial pillar: reporting, field notes, essays, commentary and investigations that clear a higher threshold and become part of a permanent professional record.

Participant-reporter

I’m not presenting myself as the expert on every subject I touch. My role is closer to a participant-reporter: enter the problem, ask better questions, gather evidence, try something when appropriate, make a decision and report what happened.

“The topics can change because the underlying life does not.”

Published work

When a piece clears the editorial threshold, it is published with a permanent URL, byline, publication date and correction trail so it can stand independently as professional work.
