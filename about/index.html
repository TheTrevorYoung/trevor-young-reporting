About Trevor

Trevor Young Reporting is where I document the life I’m building and report what I learn from it.

I’ve spent years moving between Sierra Leone and the United States, business and public-interest work, technology and real-world operations. I’m interested in what happens between an idea and the result — the parts that are usually edited out.

TYR lets me follow those questions in public. Sometimes that means using AI to solve an operating problem. Sometimes it means building an organization, testing a health goal, reporting from Sierra Leone, traveling, dealing with a setback or trying to understand why a system works the way it does.

Participant-reporter

I’m not presenting myself as the expert on every subject I touch. My role is closer to a participant-reporter: enter the problem, ask better questions, gather evidence, try something when appropriate, make a decision and report what happened.

“The topics can change because the underlying story does not.”

One life, several worlds

AI, business, health, family, travel, Sierra Leone and reporting are not meant to become separate personality brands. They are different parts of one documented life and one continuing next chapter.

Editorial note

TYR aims for meaningful transparency: show the evidence, friction, changes and relationships that matter to understanding a story without treating private life as public property.

Relevant affiliations are disclosed. Personal experience is distinguished from professional advice. Material errors are corrected. Sensitive family, financial, location, account and private-message details remain protected unless there is a clear editorial reason to publish them.
