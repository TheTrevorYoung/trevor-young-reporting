My Experiments · 30 Days

30 Days

A recurring experiment format: choose a clear question, define the protocol, establish the baseline, do the work and publish what actually happened.

Experiment One: 36 Eggs a Day

The first planned 30 Days experiment will test the famous old-school bodybuilding idea of consuming 36 eggs a day. The point is not to assume the claim works. The point is to document what happens when Trevor tries the protocol for a defined period.

The experiment will record the starting baseline, the exact protocol, daily adherence, practical difficulties, noticeable changes, relevant measurements and the final result. If the experiment is stopped, modified or fails, that becomes part of the record too.

This is a personal experiment, not medical guidance or a recommendation that other people repeat the protocol.

What 30 Days means

Future installments can test pop-culture challenges, old claims, routines, habits, fitness ideas, technology and other questions that are interesting enough to put under real-world pressure.
