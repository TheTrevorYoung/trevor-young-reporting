Editorial standards

Corrections

Getting the record right matters more than pretending we never make mistakes.

TYR corrects material errors when they are identified and verified. Minor spelling or formatting fixes may be made silently. Changes that materially affect a reader or viewer’s understanding should be noted clearly enough to explain what changed.

Correction levels

  • Cosmetic: spelling, formatting or minor wording that does not change meaning.
  • Clarification: the original was not materially false but could reasonably mislead.
  • Material correction: an error changes an important fact, conclusion or impression.
  • Retraction or withdrawal: the central claim or asset is no longer supportable or creates unacceptable harm.
